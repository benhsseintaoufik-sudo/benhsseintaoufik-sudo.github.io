DC high-voltage measurement board for electric vehicles

Group project, Feb–Jun 2024. Tools: Altium Designer.

Problem. High-voltage systems in electric vehicles need to be monitored and measured safely and accurately.

My role. I designed the schematic and the PCB layout in Altium Designer, including component placement and routing. I took electrical isolation, signal conditioning and measurement accuracy into account in the design.

Result. The board was tested and validated for its electrical and measurement performance.

Test bench for an analog neural network based on elastic waves

Laboratoire Jean Lamour, acoustics research, Jan–Apr 2026. Tools: MATLAB, oscilloscope, function generator, piezoelectric transducers.

Problem. An analog neural network that computes through elastic wave propagation needs an experimental way to measure and characterize how it responds.

My role. I built the measurement bench. I set up the piezoelectric transducers, the function generator and the oscilloscope to excite the network and record its responses. In MATLAB, I handled data acquisition, signal processing, visualization and characterization of the response.

Result. My measurements validated the behavior and performance of the proposed network architecture.

Face-recognition attendance system

Jan–Mar 2023. Tools: Python, OpenCV, Excel.

Problem. Tracking employee attendance by hand is slow and error-prone.

My role. I implemented face detection and recognition with OpenCV and image-processing techniques. I also built an automated workflow that records and organizes attendance data in Excel.

Result. I evaluated the system under different conditions to measure recognition reliability and logging accuracy.
